Real project, built and commissioned

Automated hydraulic choke manifold for FESCO

FESCO needed manual choke-manifold valves brought under automatic pressure control. As Systems Engineer on a three-person senior design team, I owned the P&ID and the PLC architecture, and served as the team's main contact with FESCO, presenting our formal design reviews.

15,000 psisystem pressure rating
11actuated valves
16tagged I/O points
15 linesbill of materials
~$18Kestimated hardware cost

What I delivered

  • P&ID and PLC architecture on Allen-Bradley ControlLogix with Studio 5000, using 4–20 mA instrumentation
  • The full AutoCAD drawing set for the system
  • Python I/O verification tooling that checks every tagged point against the design
  • A SolidWorks 3D model of the outdoor HMI stand, with wind-load simulation across weather conditions
  • The bill of materials, with specifications, quantities, selection notes and vendor sourcing for each line
